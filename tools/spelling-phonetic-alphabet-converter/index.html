Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Operational Support Segment

Why are certain numbers pronounced differently in the NATO phonetic alphabet?

Aviation and military number pronunciations (such as 'Niner' for 9, 'A-tree' for 3, and 'Fow-er' for 4) were carefully chosen by linguistic experts to overcome radio static, muffled intercoms, and language barriers. For instance, the digit 9 ('Niner') sounds distinct from the German word 'nein' (no), preventing critical misunderstandings in flight control towers.
