Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

How do I handle slash chords (e.g., C/G) when transposing?

Our transposer automatically detects slash bass notes. When you shift a chord like `C/G` up by 2 semitones, both the primary chord root (`C` becomes `D`) and the bass note (`G` becomes `A`) are transposed independently, resulting in `D/A`.

Why is choosing between sharps and flats important for musicians?

Instrument families have different physical ergonomics. String players and guitarists often prefer sharp keys (`G`, `D`, `A`) that align with open strings, while horn players and jazz pianists frequently prefer flat keys (`B♭`, `E♭`, `A♭`) due to valve and fingering configurations.
