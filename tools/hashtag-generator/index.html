What is the risk of using banned or shadowbanned hashtags?

Using spammy, repetitive, or flagged hashtags can trigger automated platform safety filters, resulting in a shadowban where your content visibility drops significantly across feeds. Our generator curates clean, standard industry tags to protect your account standing.

Is it better to place hashtags in captions or comments?

For Instagram, both caption placement and first-comment placement yield comparable algorithmic indexing results. However, placing tags in the caption ensures immediate indexing the second your post goes live, minimizing any delay in initial discovery.
