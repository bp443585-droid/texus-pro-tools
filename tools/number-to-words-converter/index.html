The Technical Core: Algorithmic Grouping and Scale Scales

When raw numerical values pass through our high-performance client-side conversion pipeline, the script breaks down the input into structured mathematical units:

1. Triplet Partitioning (The Thousands Period)

Computers process large numbers by splitting them into groups of three digits from right to left (units, thousands, millions, billions, trillions, etc.). Each 3-digit block follows the exact same internal grammar rules: hundreds, tens, and units.

2. Lexical Lookup Arrays

The engine maps digits to predefined linguistic strings: single digits (one through nine), teens (ten through nineteen), tens multiples (twenty through ninety), and magnitude scale suffixes (thousand, million, billion, trillion).

3. Decimal and Fractional Parsing

For financial computations involving cents or fractional quantities, the engine extracts digits following the decimal separator and translates them into fractional terminology (e.g., "and 45/100" or explicit decimal place names).
