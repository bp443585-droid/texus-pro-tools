Platform monetization relies heavily on advertising networks, viewer geography, audience demographics, and niche categories. Beginners often confuse CPM (Cost Per Mille) with RPM (Revenue Per Mille). CPM represents what advertisers pay YouTube per one thousand ad impressions, whereas RPM reflects what creators actually take home after platform revenue shares are deducted. Projected revenue varies dramatically across channels due to several fundamental platform variables influencing advertiser bidding:
