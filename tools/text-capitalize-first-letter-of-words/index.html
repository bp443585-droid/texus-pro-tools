Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence Case?

Sentence case capitalizes only the very first letter of the first word in a sentence (along with proper nouns), whereas Title Case (Capitalize First Letter of Words) capitalizes the initial letter of every single word in the string, regardless of its grammatical role in the sentence.
