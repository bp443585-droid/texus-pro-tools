Why Convert Excel Spreadsheets to PDF Format?

  • Fixed Page Layouts: PDFs prevent columns from spilling across awkward page breaks, locking cell dimensions and text alignment for clean reporting.

  • Universal Viewing Security: Static PDF records protect underlying formula calculations from accidental modifications while ensuring documents open smoothly on any operating system.
