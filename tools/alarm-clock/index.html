Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

Why did my online alarm not ring when my computer screen went to sleep?

When operating systems enter deep sleep or hibernation modes, they suspend CPU execution for browser tabs to save battery and power. To ensure your alarm rings reliably, adjust your computer's power and sleep settings to keep the screen active, or keep the device plugged into a power source.

Can I set multiple alarms simultaneously using this tool?

This current version supports one primary custom alarm slot with instant status feedback. You can easily reset, clear, or update your alarm time whenever your schedule shifts throughout the day.

Is my alarm time stored or transmitted to external servers?

Your privacy is fully protected. All alarm calculations, time checks, and status indicators run entirely within your local browser memory sandbox. No personal scheduling data ever leaves your device.
