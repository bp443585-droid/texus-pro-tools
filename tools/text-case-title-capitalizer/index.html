Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Editorial & Technical Guide

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes nearly all major words in a headline or title, creating visual prominence and distinct word separation. Sentence case only capitalizes the first word of the sentence and any proper nouns, mirroring standard reading paragraphs.

Which minor words are typically kept lowercase in Title Case?

Standard style guides (including APA, Chicago, and AP) generally keep articles ('a', 'an', 'the'), coordinating conjunctions ('and', 'but', 'or', 'for', 'nor'), and short prepositions ('in', 'on', 'at', 'to', 'by', 'up', 'off') in lowercase, unless they appear as the very first or last word of the title.
