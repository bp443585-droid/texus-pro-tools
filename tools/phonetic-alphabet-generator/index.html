Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

Why are certain numbers pronounced differently in aviation telephony?

Aviation number protocols modify standard numbers to prevent misinterpretation over static. For instance, '3' is pronounced 'Tree' to avoid confusion with 'three' vs 'free', '4' is 'Fower', and '9' is pronounced 'Niner' to prevent confusion with the German word 'nein' (no).
