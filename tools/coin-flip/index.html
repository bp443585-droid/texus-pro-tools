Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

Is a virtual coin toss truly random?

Yes. Modern web browsers utilize high-grade pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) seeded with system entropy. For standard decision-making and probability simulations, the outcome distribution is statistically indistinguishable from a physical coin toss.

Can I influence the outcome of the digital coin flip?

No. The outcome is determined instantly upon activation using secure mathematical functions executed within your browser. Neither user movement nor timing can manipulate the final result.

Why do I sometimes experience long streaks of Heads or Tails?

In any sequence of independent random events, streaks are entirely normal. For instance, getting five Heads in a row has a probability of `(1/2)^5`, or 1 in 32. Over hundreds of flips, the law of large numbers ensures that Heads and Tails counts will balance out closely.

Does Texus Pro Tools store my flip history or session statistics?

Never. All calculations and counter tallies run locally inside your browser's temporary memory. No personal data or session stats are ever sent to external servers, ensuring complete privacy.
