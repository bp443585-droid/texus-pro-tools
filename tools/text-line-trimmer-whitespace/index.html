Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

What is the difference between leading and trailing whitespace?

Leading whitespace refers to spaces, tabs, and indentation characters appearing at the *beginning* of a text line. Trailing whitespace consists of invisible spaces lingering at the *end* of a line after the final visible character. Both are typically unintended artifacts of copy-pasting and can cause severe validation errors in programming languages like Python or YAML.

Does this tool modify my original text on a server?

No. All text processing is executed 100% client-side inside your browser’s JavaScript execution environment. Your data strings never travel across external networks or get logged to external databases, ensuring complete data privacy.
