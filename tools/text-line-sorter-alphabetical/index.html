Capital letters have lower numeric code points than lowercase letters in standard ASCII tables, making case-sensitive and case-insensitive settings critical for accurate alphabetical alignment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Core Diagnostic Segment

Why do uppercase letters sometimes appear before lowercase letters when sorting?

By default, standard computer character encoding (ASCII) assigns lower numerical values to uppercase letters (A-Z = 65–90) than to lowercase letters (a-z = 97–122). If you prefer case-insensitive sorting where "Apple" and "apple" are treated identically regardless of capitalization, ensure the case-sensitive toggle is adjusted or use natural collation.
